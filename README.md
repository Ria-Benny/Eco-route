### Move with less impact.

**EcoRoute** helps you compare driving routes not just by **time and distance, but also by their environmental impact**.

Two routes can take you to the same destination while producing different amounts of fuel consumption and vehicle emissions. EcoRoute makes this trade-off visible by comparing **fastest, cleanest, and balanced routes** and estimating their **CO₂ and NO₂ emissions** based on your selected vehicle.

> **A drive is more than just minutes. Choose a route that considers the planet too.**

---

## Why EcoRoute?

Most navigation systems focus primarily on getting you to your destination as quickly as possible.

But traffic, distance, stop-and-go driving, and route conditions can influence how much fuel a vehicle consumes and how much pollution it produces.

**EcoRoute AI adds emissions to the equation.**

It helps you understand the trade-off between **speed and environmental impact**, giving you another factor to consider before you start your journey.

## How It Works

### 1. Choose your route

Select your **starting point and destination** on the Kochi map.

### 2. Select your vehicle

Choose between a **2-wheeler or 4-wheeler** and select the relevant model year.

### 3. Compare your options

Review the available route choices based on:

* Travel time
* Distance
* CO₂ emissions
* NO₂ emissions

### 4. Make your choice

Choose the route that best matches your priority — **speed, lower emissions, or a balance between the two.**

---

## A Smarter Way to Think About Travel

EcoRoute AI doesn't tell you which route you *must* take.

Instead, it gives you another piece of information to consider:

**How much does my journey cost the environment?**

By making emissions visible alongside travel time, EcoRoute encourages more informed and environmentally conscious travel decisions.

---

##  A Note on Accuracy

EcoRoute AI is an **educational and route-planning tool**, not a replacement for real-time navigation or vehicle telemetry.

The CO₂ and NO₂ values shown are **indicative estimates** modeled using factors such as:

* Route distance
* Estimated traffic conditions
* Stop-and-start driving
* Vehicle type
* Driving patterns

The estimates are **not measured directly from your vehicle** and should not be interpreted as real-time emissions data.
